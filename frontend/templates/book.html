New booking

Creates a booking, adds the flight, and issues the ticket in one step (sp_new_booking). If any rule rejects it, nothing is saved.

Ticket an existing booking

Sells a seat on one flight of a booking (sp_book_ticket). A Pending booking becomes Confirmed. Try a seat that is already sold to see the database refuse it.

Suggestions are the seats still available on the chosen flight.

Add a connecting flight

Adds the next leg to a booking (sp_add_leg). A trigger checks that it starts at the airport where the previous leg ended and leaves after it lands. Then issue a ticket for it on the previous tab.
